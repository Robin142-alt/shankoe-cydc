Outcomes Framework

Before & After Transformation Trajectory

Illustrating the systemic shift catalyzed by Shankoe CYDC across Narok County.

Context & Challenges

Baseline Community Hurdle

  • • High rates of school dropout due to pastoralist vulnerabilities and economic shocks.
  • • Limited vocational training hubs or modern market skills for adolescent youth.
  • • Stigmatised or unavailable psychosocial and mental health support.
  • • Vulnerability of households to recurrent climatic droughts and pasture loss.
Targeted Transformation

Sustained Community Outcome

  • ✓ Safe learning environments with supported retention and literacy success.
  • ✓ Employable youth running viable local businesses with entrepreneurial confidence.
  • ✓ De-stigmatised mental wellbeing dialogues and accessible peer counseling.
  • ✓ Community-led reforestation, water protection, and adaptive climate capacity.
