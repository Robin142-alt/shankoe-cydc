Who We Are

Rooted in Narok County, Dedicated to Holistic Development

Shankoe Methodist Child and Youth Development Centre (Shankoe CYDC) works directly with vulnerable children, young people, caregivers, and local leaders to build environments where every child is protected and empowered to reach their God-given potential.

Established under the Methodist Church in Kenya, we address rural and pastoralist challenges by integrating foundational education, youth vocational capability, mental wellbeing, child rights protection, and ecological resilience.

Our Vision

“A world where every child and young person is safe, valued, and equipped to reach their full potential.”

Our Mission

“To embrace, engage, and empower vulnerable children and young people through education, health, and skills development, enabling them to thrive.”

Programmatic Pathways

Integrated initiatives designed to support children and youth across every developmental milestone while strengthening families and communities.

Education & Lifelong Learning

Expanding opportunities for children to access inclusive quality primary and secondary schooling, remedial literacy, study spaces, and educational retention support.

Our Methodology

Theory of Change

Our systemic model connects investments in education, protection, and youth capability with stronger community accountability—ensuring children grow in safer environments with lasting economic and developmental security.

Core Premise:

When communities are supported with resources, knowledge, and protective systems, generational outcomes are sustained from within.

Our Impact & Measurement

Shankoe CYDC is dedicated to transparent stewardship and evidence-based reporting. Our Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) framework tracks children enrolled, youth equipped, caregivers mobilized, and community safeguards resolved.

Stewardship Standard:

Rigorous data collection, child safeguarding compliance, and verified reporting across all donor and partner investments.
