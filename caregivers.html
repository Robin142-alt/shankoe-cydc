Caregiver Engagement & Family Strengthening

Children thrive when families are supported. Our community-centered architecture works alongside parents, guardians, and local leaders to build protective, resilient households.

Beyond the Individual Child

Why Caregiver Support is at the Core of Our Strategy

Shankoe CYDC firmly believes that an intervention focusing solely on children in isolation is only half the solution. True, lasting transformation requires that parents, guardians, and community elders are active allies.

In rural and pastoralist Narok County, families navigate climate shocks, economic hardships, and social pressures. By creating dedicated spaces for caregiver dialogue, positive parenting, and mental health awareness, we empower families to build nurturing, resilient homes.

Guiding Principle:

“When you strengthen a caregiver with knowledge and emotional support, you protect an entire generation.”

Pillars of Family Strengthening

Caregiver Engagement Areas

Designed to support families through every developmental phase of childhood and adolescence.

Caregiver Meetings & Peer Circles

Regular community-based gatherings where mothers, fathers, and guardians share experiences, address child-rearing challenges, and build mutual support networks.

Programme Framework

Positive Parenting Support

Practical workshops on non-violent discipline, active listening, child emotional development, and adolescent communication, reducing stress in the household.

Programme Framework

Child Safeguarding Education

Sensitising caregivers on children’s legal rights, recognising signs of abuse, preventing early marriage or school dropout, and safe reporting mechanisms.

Programme Framework

Mental Wellbeing Awareness

Demystifying mental health and psychosocial struggles. Equipping caregivers to notice anxiety, depression, and trauma in adolescents with empathy.

Programme Framework

Community Dialogue & Barazas

Facilitating constructive forums bringing together local elders, church leaders, county child protection officers, and families to address systemic community hurdles.

Programme Framework

Livelihood & Economic Links

Exploring community savings clubs, financial literacy, and climate-smart household resilience initiatives if confirmed by Shankoe CYDC leadership.
