About Shankoe CYDC

Learn about our organisational foundation, guiding mission, core values, strategic objectives, and evidence-informed Theory of Change.

Who We Are

A Faithful Dedication to Every Child & Youth

Shankoe Methodist Child and Youth Development Centre (Shankoe CYDC) was established under the auspices of the Methodist Church in Kenya to address critical vulnerabilities affecting children and adolescents in Narok County.

Our approach brings together faith, community engagement, and proven development methodologies. We work in direct partnership with caregivers, schools, elders, and youth to foster safe spaces, quality learning, holistic health, and environmental sustainability.

Child Safeguarding Commitment

Shankoe CYDC maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against child abuse, neglect, exploitation, or discrimination. Every programme and activity prioritises the safety and supreme welfare of the child.

Our Guiding Compass

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

“A world where every child and young person is safe, valued, and equipped to reach their full potential.”

We envision an equitable society where no child's background limits their future, and every adolescent possesses the dignity and tools to thrive.

Our Mission

“To embrace, engage, and empower vulnerable children and young people through education, health, and skills development, enabling them to thrive.”

Our day-to-day mission translates into compassionate, tangible support across education, health, mentorship, and sustainable community empowerment.

Ethical Foundation

Our Core Values

Six enduring pillars shaping our interactions, interventions, and partnerships.

Inclusion

No child or youth is left behind. We actively reach children with disabilities, vulnerable households, and marginalised pastoralist communities.

Integrity

Transparency and accountability in all actions, stewardship of resources, and honest reporting to partners, donors, and the community.

Empowerment

Building confidence, resilience, and capability. We equip young people and caregivers to be agents of their own uplifting change.

Resilience

Overcoming adversity through adaptability and innovation, especially in the face of climatic, social, and economic shocks.

Social Justice

Promoting equality, human dignity, and fairness. Advocating tirelessly for the fundamental rights and protections of every child.

Partnership

Leveraging collaboration for greater community impact. Believing that collective effort creates deeper, more enduring results.

Strategic Roadmap

Strategic Objectives

The organisation works strategically to achieve five overarching long-term objectives:

1

Inclusive Quality Education

Improve access to inclusive quality education and lifelong learning opportunities for vulnerable children and youth.

2

Employable Skills & Entrepreneurship

Empower young people with employable practical skills and entrepreneurship capacity to foster sustainable livelihoods.

3

Psychosocial & Mental Health Support

Enhance access to psychosocial and mental health support, emotional resilience, and peer counseling systems.

4

Rights Awareness & Advocacy

Build community awareness and advocacy for children and young people's rights, protection, and dignity.

5

Community Climate Resilience

Strengthen community climate resilience through ecological literacy, indigenous conservation, and adaptive action.

Operational Pillars

Key Strategies

To achieve these strategic objectives, Shankoe CYDC deploys five core strategies:

Protection of Children & Young People’s Rights

Institutionalising safeguarding policies, rapid response channels, and awareness campaigns across homes and schools.

Skills Development

Delivering practical, competency-based technical and entrepreneurial training aligned with rural economic opportunities.

Policy Advocacy

Collaborating with local and national government agencies, educators, and civil society to enforce child-friendly policies.

Community Mobilisation

Empowering local elders, women's groups, and caregivers to champion positive parenting and participatory development.

Building Strategic Partnerships

Establishing collaborative networks with churches, NGOs, donors, and academic hubs for resource and knowledge sharing.

Logic Model

Theory of Change

A clear, structured overview demonstrating how strategic inputs translate into sustainable change across Narok County.

IF WE INVEST IN

Integrated Interventions

Shankoe CYDC invests in strengthening community child protection and climate resilience systems, expanding education access, empowering young people, and promoting mental wellbeing,

Core Inputs: Safe spaces, scholarships, mentor networks, mental health literacy, seedling nurseries, community dialogues.
THEN

Direct Beneficiary Outcomes

children and young people in Narok County will grow in safer, inclusive environments with better life outcomes,

Measurable Shift: Reduced dropout rates, improved psychosocial wellbeing, viable youth businesses, zero child rights violations.
BECAUSE

Community Sustainability

communities will be more accountable, supportive, and capable of sustaining change.

Underlying Driver: Local ownership, trained caregivers, empowered youth champions, and institutional Methodist stewardship.

Approach to Community Development

Shankoe CYDC rejects top-down development models. Instead, we utilise asset-based community development (ABCD) where local wisdom, pastoralist resilience, and cultural leadership are respected as vital strengths. We work with communities rather than merely for them, ensuring interventions continue to bear fruit across generations.
