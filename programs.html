Our Programmes & Action Areas

Holistic, rights-based interventions designed to protect children, empower adolescents, support caregivers, and build resilient communities in Narok County.

Program Area 01 Active Framework

Education & Lifelong Learning

Expanding opportunities for vulnerable children and adolescents in Narok County to access inclusive, equitable, and quality basic education, foundational literacy, and remedial academic support.

Key Objectives

Improve enrollment and retention rates for out-of-school and vulnerable children, provide learning materials, and support school re-entry pathways.

Programme Components & Activities:

  • • Remedial learning clubs and reading literacy circles.
  • • Education kit provision and school-readiness support.
  • • Advocacy against educational dropouts among pastoralist youth.
  • • Future Expansion School infrastructure and digital learning links.
Target Group: School-age children & adolescent learners
Location: Narok County, Kenya

Program Area 02 Active Framework

Youth Skills & Entrepreneurship

Equipping young men and women with practical vocational skills, financial literacy, market-aligned entrepreneurship training, and leadership confidence for sustainable self-reliance.

Key Objectives

Bridge the youth employment divide through technical competency, apprentice placements, and startup incubation support.

Programme Components & Activities:

  • • Hands-on technical and artisan trade orientations.
  • • Financial literacy, savings group training, and enterprise coaching.
  • • Youth mentorship circles and career guidance days.
  • • Future Pathway Seed funding & business startup starter kits.
Target Group: Youth aged 15–25, out-of-school youth
Focus: Employability & Enterprise

Program Area 03 Active Framework

Mental Health & Psychosocial Support

Fostering emotional wellbeing, trauma resilience, safe listening spaces, and destigmatised psychosocial care for young people facing adversity.

Key Objectives

Equip young people with coping mechanisms, reduce mental health stigma in pastoralist areas, and link individuals in distress to confidential counseling.

Programme Components & Activities:

  • • Safe peer-to-peer reflection circles and psychoeducation.
  • • Community mental wellbeing awareness workshops.
  • • Caregiver guidance on adolescent emotional distress.
  • • Referral linkages to verified healthcare professionals.

Program Area 04 Core Mandate

Child & Youth Protection

Creating uncompromisingly safe environments where children's fundamental rights, bodily integrity, and dignity are protected against all forms of violence, exploitation, and abuse.

Key Objectives

Enforce stringent child safeguarding protocols across centre activities, establish early detection mechanisms, and coordinate with government child protection authorities.

Programme Components & Activities:

  • • Child safeguarding code of conduct training for staff and volunteers.
  • • Community child rights reporting awareness channels.
  • • Protective clubs empowering children to know and speak their rights.
  • • Safe emergency response and legal protection referrals.

Program Area 05 Active Framework

Community Advocacy & Mobilisation

Empowering grassroots community members, church congregations, and local leaders to actively champion child welfare, gender equality, and social justice.

Key Objectives

Mobilise collective action against harmful practices, foster supportive parenting, and build a unified community coalition for child wellbeing.

Programme Components & Activities:

  • • Community barazas (dialogues) with elders, parents, and youth.
  • • Inter-faith and church community advocacy sermons.
  • • Parenting and caregiver engagement circles.
  • • Policy advocacy with county-level stakeholders in Narok.

Program Area 06 Active Framework

Community Climate Resilience

Building environmental stewardship and adaptive capacity in Narok County to protect vulnerable families and children from the impacts of droughts and climate shocks.

Key Objectives

Promote youth-led ecological action, tree planting, indigenous water conservation awareness, and sustainable land care.

Programme Components & Activities:

  • • Tree planting campaigns and school eco-club nurseries.
  • • Climate resilience education integrated into youth training.
  • • Water conservation and drought adaptation dialogues.
  • • Youth green entrepreneurship opportunities.
