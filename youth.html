Next Generation Leadership

Youth Empowerment & Skills Hub

Building confidence, employable capability, and entrepreneurial resilience for young people across Narok County, Kenya.

Aspirational Pathway

From Vulnerability to Visionary Leadership

Young people in Narok County possess extraordinary potential, resilience, and creativity. Shankoe CYDC creates a platform where their talents are cultivated through market-relevant technical skills, mentorship, leadership development, and innovation.

Whether a young person seeks to master a trade, start a local enterprise, advocate for community rights, or mentor younger peers, our Youth Hub provides structured, encouraging pathways.

Comprehensive Framework

The 8 Pillars of Youth Empowerment

An energetic, holistic ecosystem nurturing every dimension of youth advancement.

Skills Development

Practical, hands-on vocational modules tailored to emerging opportunities in trades, technology, and agribusiness.

Employability

CV writing, interview preparation, digital literacy, workplace ethics, and professional communication skills.

Entrepreneurship

Business idea validation, financial recordkeeping, local market assessment, and micro-enterprise incubation.

Mentorship

Pairing youth with experienced professionals, church role models, and community leaders for one-on-one coaching.

Leadership

Civic engagement, community organizing, ethical decision-making, and representation in youth consultative councils.

Life Skills & Resilience

Critical thinking, emotional intelligence, stress management, conflict resolution, and healthy relationship building.

Innovation & Green Action

Grassroots technological solutions, digital tools, and climate adaptation innovations tailored for rural Kenya.

Opportunities & Linkages

Connecting graduates with vocational bursaries, apprenticeships, cooperative networks, and startup fairs.
